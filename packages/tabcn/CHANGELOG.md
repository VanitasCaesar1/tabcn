# tabcn

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- 25f8144: Bottom components. Sheets: `TabPanel` `snapPoints` (and the `SnapPoint` type), `snap` and `snapTo` on `useTabPanel`, and `PanelScrollView` in `tabcn/gestures` (`PanelDragArea`'s `panel` is now optional inside a panel); while a screen reader is on, sheets open only to their highest snap point. Companions: `TabBarCompanion` places a control against the bar and moves with it. Toasts: `useTabBarInset()` gives the bottom offset above everything tabcn draws, from anywhere under the `TabBarProvider`. Sub-tabs: `TabButton` `onLongPress`, offered to screen readers as a named `longpress` action. `TabButton` now merges a passed `accessibilityState` with its own `selected` instead of replacing it. Opening a panel now covers a peeking panel on the same anchor instead of hiding it: the peeking one (a mini player under a sheet) keeps its state and its reserved height and comes back when the open one rests. `react-native-safe-area-context` is now an optional peer, read by the adapters (both navigators already require it).

  Sheet navigation: an empty (zero-height) floating bar no longer tucks panels 16 pt behind it, so a sheet can stand in for the bar (the Section Sheet design) with a visible strip that matches the reported height. Scrubbing: `TabScrub` in `tabcn/gestures` is a `TabList` you can press and slide along to move through the tabs; `TabIndicator` follows the finger inside it. Live tabs: `useLiveTab(routeName)` (and the `LiveTab` type) shares a progress value and a line of text per tab, so a screen or a service can drive a ring or a countdown on a tab without re-rendering the navigator or the bar.
- ff870a7: Minimize on scroll (`useMinimizeOnScroll`, `useTabBarMinimized`, `useRestoreTabBar`, `TabBarRoot minimizeBehavior`), a rolling `BadgeCount`, and a side rail: `TabBarRoot placement="rail"` plus `TabBarState.position` read from the navigator's `tabBarPosition`.
- ff870a7: Panels: `TabBarProvider`, `TabPanel`, `useTabPanel`, `usePanelAnchor`, `useTabBarOverride` (from `tabcn/expo-router` and `tabcn/react-navigation`), and the optional `tabcn/gestures` entry with `PanelDragArea` (needs `react-native-gesture-handler`). Apps without a `TabBarProvider` see no change.
